Python Snippet: Training a Tokenizer

from tokenizers import ByteLevelBPETokenizer

# Initialize BPE Tokenizer
tokenizer = ByteLevelBPETokenizer()

# Train on custom corpus file
tokenizer.train(
    files=["dataset_cleaned.txt"],
    vocab_size=32768,
    min_frequency=2,
    special_tokens=["<|endoftext|>"]
)

# Save vocabulary and merger rules
tokenizer.save_model("./my_tokenizer")

Stage 4 Output: Base Model

Raw Completion

A base model only performs next-token completion. If you ask it a question, it writes another question instead of answering.

User: "What is the capital of France?"
Base Model: " What is the capital of Germany? What is the capital of Spain? List 10 European capitals..."

Stage 5 Output: SFT & DPO Aligned

Chat Assistant

After Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on dialog pairs and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), the model directly answers queries.

User: "What is the capital of France?"
Assistant: "The capital of France is Paris. It is known for cultural landmarks like the Eiffel Tower and Louvre Museum."
